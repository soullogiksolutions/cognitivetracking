Our Mission

At Soul Logik, we combine microfactory innovation, sustainable design, and scientific personalization to redefine activewear. Using upcycled, biodegradable fabrics crafted locally, our mission is to minimize environmental impact while maximizing your performance with gear tailored from personalized data insights.

  • Microfactory Manufacturing: Small-batch, local production reduces waste and carbon footprint, delivering quality you can trust.
  • Eco-Friendly Materials: Biodegradable, upcycled fibers engineered for durability, comfort, and microplastic reduction.
  • Scientific Personalization: Personalized quizzes and assessments analyze your cognitive and physical data points to inform apparel design tailored to your needs.
  • Performance & Comfort: Innovatively designed activewear optimized for breathability, sweat control, and durability based on your unique profile.
  • Future-Ready Innovation: Our personalized quiz platform is the first step toward launching a fully customized, scientifically designed sportswear line.

What makes microfactory production special?

Our microfactories enable local, small-batch production that dramatically reduces waste and emissions compared to traditional manufacturing.

We use data points collected from personalized quizzes assessing cognitive and physical traits to guide fabric choice, fit, and functionality in future custom products.

No! It wasn't a typo. There are many benefits to understanding how clothing affects our behavior, emotions and decision making. Dopamine dressing involves wearing clothing to boost happiness and well-being for instance. Therefore, understanding your personality helps us customize the clothing.
